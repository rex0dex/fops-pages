<div style="overflow-x: auto;" markdown="1">

Snacks, daubers, and free coffee are available. Credit cards are accepted.

## Rules

- Numbers are called every 13 seconds.
- Players must be 18 years or older.
- Please be respectful of Bingo volunteers.
- Management reserves the right to refuse service.
